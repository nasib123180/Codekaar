What is QMR

More than a name — a movement.

QMR stands for Quick Modern Revolution. It's our answer to a simple question: what if young Afghans built the tech they want to see in the world?

We started in Khost, Afghanistan — a city full of young talent but short on opportunities. Instead of waiting for someone else to build the tools, platforms, and businesses we wanted, we decided to build them ourselves.

Today QMR is a community of builders — developers, designers, students and creators. We share knowledge, collaborate on real projects, and help each other grow. Two of our biggest projects so far are CodeKaar (a web studio) and Pashtoons (a social platform — coming soon).

We believe in learning by building. Every member who joins QMR doesn't just learn to code — they learn to ship real things for real people.

How QMR started

From a small idea in Khost to a growing community of builders.

1
The Spark

A small group of friends

We were students in Khost who loved technology but had no place to learn it. We started meeting up, sharing resources, and coding together.

2
The Name

Quick Modern Revolution

We gave our group a name that matched its spirit — fast, modern, and a real change from the old way of doing things.

3
The Builders

CodeKaar is born

We built our own web studio — CodeKaar — to help local businesses in Afghanistan get online with fast, beautiful websites.

4
The Platform
