What is Pashtoons

A home for our community online.

A place where Pashto speakers can connect, share, and be themselves — without translation, without compromise.

Most social platforms were built for English speakers first — everyone else is an afterthought. Pashtoons is different. It's designed from day one for our language, our culture, and our community.

Built by the team behind CodeKaar and QMR in Khost, Afghanistan — Pashtoons brings together everything you'd expect from a modern social app, but with a Pashto-first experience.

Posts, real-time chat, friends, likes, comments, shares — all in one place. And it's coming soon.
