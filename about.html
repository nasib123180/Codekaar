Who's behind CodeKaar

I'm CodeKaar.

A self-taught web studio from Alisher, Khost. I started CodeKaar because I saw too many good local businesses struggling online — either with no website, or with one that looked stuck in 2010.

I build fast, modern, mobile-first websites that actually bring customers. No templates, no filler — just clean design that gets out of the way of your business.

When I'm not building client sites, I'm working on my own products — Pashtoons, a social platform for our community — or helping other young developers through QMR.

Our Story

How CodeKaar started

1
The Spark

A laptop and curiosity

It started with a simple laptop and the realization that the internet is the biggest opportunity Afghanistan has ever had — if we know how to use it.

2
Learning

Teaching myself to code

No bootcamp, no course fees — just free tutorials, late nights, and building things over and over until they worked.

3
First Client

A real business, a real site

My first client was a small local business. When their phone started ringing from the website, I knew this was what I wanted to do.

4
CodeKaar

A studio is born

I gave the work a name — CodeKaar — and turned a side project into a real studio helping businesses across Afghanistan get online.

5
Today

Part of something bigger

CodeKaar is now part of QMR — a community of young builders in Khost working together on tech projects for our generation.

CodeKaar is part of QMR.

QMR (Quick Modern Revolution) is a community of young tech builders in Khost. We train, we build, and we grow together. CodeKaar and Pashtoons are our two flagship projects — proof that world-class software can come from home.
